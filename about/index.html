About & FAQ

ShipIt Sunday is a free weekly challenge for people who build with AI. We post a small app or game every Friday, you ship it by Sunday, and on Monday we celebrate everything that shipped.

Frequently asked questions

What is ShipIt Sunday?

ShipIt Sunday is a free weekly vibe-coding challenge. Every Friday we post a small app or game idea plus a starter template; you build it over the weekend with an AI coding tool and submit by Sunday night (Pacific Time).

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The challenges are designed for AI coding tools like Cursor, Claude Code, Bolt, Lovable, v0, Replit or Copilot. A starter prompt is included every week.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Entering is free and there are no paid tiers right now.

Which AI tools are allowed?

Any of them. Most of your project should be AI-written, and libraries such as Three.js or Phaser are fine.

How do I submit?

Before Sunday 11:59pm PT, either open a submission issue on our GitHub repo with your live link, or post your link on X with #ShipItSunday.

Are there prizes?

Not cash yet. Every valid entry is featured in Monday's showcase and newsletter, with fun awards like Most Fun and Best Single Prompt.

Who owns what I build?

You do. By submitting, you let us feature your project's name, link, screenshot and prompt with credit.

Can my company sponsor a week?

Yes. Sponsors can theme a week around their tool and fund prizes. Use the sponsor contact on the home page.

Contact & sponsorship

Message @shipitsunday on X or open an issue on GitHub. A sponsor email will be added at launch.
